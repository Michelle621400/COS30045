About this project

WattWise is a small teaching example built to demonstrate a simple three-page website with clear navigation, using appliance energy consumption in the Australian market as its subject matter.

What's real, what's placeholder

The layout, navigation and structure are the actual deliverable. The figures on the Televisions page are sample data included to show how content could be presented — they are not drawn from a specific retailer or product.

How it's built

Home, Televisions and About Us are three separate HTML pages that share one stylesheet and one small script. The script highlights whichever nav link matches the page you're on and keeps the footer year current.
