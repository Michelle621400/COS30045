Why this matters in Australia

Electricity prices and household energy use are a regular topic of conversation across Australian homes. Screen size, panel technology and how many hours a television runs each day all change how much it costs to own — not just to buy. This site walks through the basics using the television as a worked example, with sample figures you can compare against your own bill.
