Why this matters in Australia

Electricity prices and household energy use are a regular topic of conversation across Australian homes. Screen size, panel technology and how many hours a television runs each day all change how much it costs to own — not just to buy. This site walks through the basics using the television as a worked example, with sample figures you can compare against your own bill.

Comparing television energy use

Three placeholder tips

  • Turn off fully at the wall rather than leaving a television on standby overnight.
  • Match screen size to the room — a larger panel run at low brightness can still use less than a smaller one at full brightness.
  • Check the star rating label before buying; it's designed for side-by-side comparison.

About this project

WattWise is a small teaching example built to demonstrate a simple three-page website with JavaScript-driven navigation, using appliance energy consumption in the Australian market as its subject matter.

What's real, what's placeholder

The layout, navigation and structure are the actual deliverable. The figures on the Televisions page are sample data included to show how content could be presented — they are not drawn from a specific retailer or product.

How it's built

One shared HTML page holds all three sections; a small JavaScript file shows and hides them and updates the navigation to reflect the current page, so no reload is needed when moving between Home, Televisions and About Us.
